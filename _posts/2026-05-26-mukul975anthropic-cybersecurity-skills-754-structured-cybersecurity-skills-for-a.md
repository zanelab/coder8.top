The field of AI-assisted cybersecurity has been evolving rapidly, with developers increasingly integrating AI coding assistants into their security workflows. ## Repository Overview

**Anthropic-Cybersecurity-Skills** is a carefully curated collection of 754 structured cybersecurity skills specifically designed for AI agents. The repository stands out for its rigorous approach to organizing cybersecurity knowledge in a format that AI systems can effectively understand and utilize. Each skill is meticulously mapped to established industry frameworks, ensuring that the knowledge base aligns with globally recognized cybersecurity standards and best practices.

### Key Features

The repository offers several distinctive features that make it particularly valuable for security professionals and AI developers:

**Multi-Framework Mapping**: One of the most impressive aspects of this repository is its comprehensive mapping to five major cybersecurity frameworks:
- **MITRE ATT&CK**: The Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge framework, which provides a comprehensive matrix of adversary tactics and techniques
- **NIST CSF 2.0**: The National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework version 2.0, offering a structured approach to managing cybersecurity risk
- **MITRE ATLAS**: The Adversarial Threat Landscape for Artificial Intelligence Systems, specifically addressing AI-specific threats
- **D3FEND**: A knowledge base of defensive cybersecurity countermeasures
- **NIST AI RMF**: The NIST AI Risk Management Framework, addressing artificial intelligence-specific risk considerations

**Broad Platform Compatibility**: The skills are designed to work seamlessly with a wide range of AI coding assistants and development platforms, including Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Codex CLI, Cursor, Gemini CLI, and over 20 additional platforms. This extensive compatibility ensures that security teams can integrate these skills regardless of their preferred development environment.

## Technical Architecture

### Skill Structure

Each of the 754 skills follows a standardized format that promotes consistency and reusability. The skills are organized across 26 distinct security domains, covering the full spectrum of cybersecurity concerns from network security to application security, incident response, and threat intelligence.

The **agentskills.io** standard is used as the foundational format for skill representation, which provides a structured approach to defining skill metadata, capabilities, and integration requirements. This standardization enables AI agents to consistently interpret and apply the skills across different contexts and platforms.

### Framework Integration

The mapping to multiple frameworks serves several important purposes:

1. **Interoperability**: By aligning with established standards, skills can be easily understood by security professionals familiar with traditional cybersecurity frameworks
2. **Comprehensive Coverage**: Different frameworks emphasize different aspects of security; the multi-framework approach ensures that no critical security domain is overlooked
3. **AI-Optimized Knowledge Representation**: While the frameworks themselves are designed for human consumption, the skill mappings have been optimized for AI interpretation and application
